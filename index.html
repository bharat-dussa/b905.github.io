Notes for the carpenter

  • Office door opens into the office. The two doors are less than a foot apart.
  • Wardrobe shutters are hinged, not sliding, so the pull-outs can open fully.
  • Ironing board in the wardrobe must be a swivel type. A straight pull-out hits the bed.
  • Kitchen upper cabinets start about 7 ft from the floor.
  • Fridge space is about 27 inches wide. Measure before buying.
  • Use branded soft-close drawer channels. Saree trays are heavy.
  • Fit safety grills on all large windows before moving in.
  • Fix the TV unit and wardrobes to the wall so they cannot tip.
